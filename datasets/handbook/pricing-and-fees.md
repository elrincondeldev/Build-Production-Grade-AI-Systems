# Pricing and Fees

| Plan | Monthly fee | Free ATM withdrawals / month | FX markup | Virtual cards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | €0 | 2 (then €1.50 each) | 1.0% | 1 |
| Plus | €4.99 | 5 | 0.5% | 5 |
| Premium | €12.99 | Unlimited | 0% | Unlimited |

## Other fees

- Card replacement: **free** for fraud cases, **€7** for lost or damaged cards on Basic, free on Plus and Premium.
- Instant transfer to external bank: **0.3%** (minimum €0.30).
- Inactivity fee: none.
- Weekend FX: an extra **0.5%** markup applies to currency exchanges made between Friday 23:00 and Sunday 23:00 CET on Basic and Plus.

Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, except upgrades, which are immediate.
