# Refund Policy

Kestrel Pay customers can request a refund for **card fees** and **account fees** charged by Kestrel Pay. Refunds for purchases made *with* a Kestrel card must be requested from the merchant first (see the Card Dispute Process).

## Eligibility

- Fee refunds can be requested within **60 days** of the charge.
- The monthly Premium plan fee is refunded **pro rata** if you downgrade within the first **14 days** of a billing cycle.
- ATM fees charged by third-party ATM operators are **not** refundable by Kestrel Pay.
- Foreign exchange (FX) markups are refundable only if the FX rate applied was more than **2%** above the reference rate published that day.

## How to request a refund

1. Open the Kestrel app → **Help** → **Fees and refunds**.
2. Select the charge and choose a reason.
3. Support reviews requests within **3 business days**.

Approved refunds are returned to the original Kestrel account within **5 business days**. Customers who cancel their account receive approved refunds by bank transfer to an account in their name.

## Limits

A customer can receive at most **3 goodwill refunds per calendar year**. Goodwill refunds are refunds granted outside the rules above at the discretion of a Support team lead.
