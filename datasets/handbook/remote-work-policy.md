# Remote Work Policy

Kestrel Pay is **hybrid-first**. Employees work from the office at least **2 days per week**, chosen with their team. Teams agree on one shared "anchor day" per week.

- Fully remote arrangements need approval from a **director** and the People Team.
- Employees can work from another country for up to **30 days per calendar year**. Longer stays require a tax and legal review requested **6 weeks** in advance.
- The home-office stipend is **€500 once** at hiring plus **€30 per month** for internet costs, paid with salary.
- Company laptops must not be used on public Wi-Fi without the corporate VPN.
