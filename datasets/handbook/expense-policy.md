# Expense Policy

Submit expenses in the Expenses tool within **30 days** of the purchase, with a receipt for anything above **€25**.

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals while travelling | €60 per day |
| Client dinners | €90 per person, manager approval required |
| Hotels | €180 per night (€250 in London, Paris, New York) |
| Flights | Economy for flights under 6 hours |
| Learning budget | €1,200 per year per employee (courses, books, conferences) |

Expenses above **€1,000** need approval from a director. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary if approved before the **20th**.
