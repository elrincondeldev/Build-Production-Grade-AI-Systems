# On-Call Handbook

- Rotations last **one week**, Monday 10:00 to Monday 10:00 CET, with a primary and a secondary engineer.
- Acknowledge pages within **5 minutes**. If the primary does not acknowledge, the page escalates to the secondary after 5 minutes and to the engineering manager after 15.
- On-call compensation: **€300 per week** plus **€50** for every page handled outside working hours.
- After a night with pages after 00:00, the on-call engineer starts work late the next day.
- Every alert must link to a runbook. An alert without a runbook is a bug: file it.
