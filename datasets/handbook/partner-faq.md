# Partner Merchant FAQ

**How long do settlements take?**
Card payments to partner merchants settle in **2 business days** (T+2).

**What is the merchant discount rate?**
The standard rate is **1.4% + €0.10** per card transaction for EU cards.

**How do partners get support?**
Through the partner portal ticketing system. Response time target: **1 business day**.

**Can partners offer Kestrel Pay cashback?**
Yes, through the cashback programme. Partners fund the cashback; Kestrel Pay handles the payout.
