# Engineering Salary Bands 2026 (Spain, gross annual)

| Level | Title | Band |
|---|---|---|
| L3 | Engineer | €42,000 – €55,000 |
| L4 | Senior Engineer | €55,000 – €72,000 |
| L5 | Staff Engineer | €72,000 – €95,000 |
| L6 | Principal Engineer | €95,000 – €120,000 |

Promotions are decided in the **April** and **October** calibration cycles. Equity refreshers are granted in April.
