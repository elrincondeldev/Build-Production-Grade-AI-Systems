# Performance Review Process

Reviews happen twice a year, in **March** and **September**. Each review includes a self-review, feedback from **2–4 peers** and a manager assessment. Ratings: *Below expectations*, *Meets*, *Exceeds*, *Outstanding*. Calibration across teams happens one month later, in April and October.

Employees rated *Below expectations* twice in a row enter a **60-day** improvement plan with weekly check-ins.
