Security FAQ for Customers

Will Kestrel Pay ever ask for my PIN?

No. Kestrel Pay staff will never ask for your app PIN, full card number, CVV or one-time passcodes, by phone, email or chat.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication?

Go to Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication. We support authenticator apps and passkeys. SMS codes are available as a fallback only.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Log in from the web at app.kestrelpay.example and choose Lock all devices. All sessions are revoked within 60 seconds.
