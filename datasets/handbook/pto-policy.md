# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

- Full-time employees get **25 days** of paid vacation per year plus local public holidays.
- Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on **31 March**.
- Sick leave does not reduce vacation days. A doctor's note is required after **3 consecutive** sick days.
- Parental leave: **20 weeks** fully paid for the primary caregiver and **8 weeks** for the secondary caregiver.
- Request vacation in the HR tool at least **2 weeks** in advance for absences longer than 3 days.
