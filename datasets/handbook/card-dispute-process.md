# Card Dispute Process

A **dispute** (also called a chargeback request) is how a customer challenges a card transaction they do not recognise or that the merchant did not honour.

## Before opening a dispute

Customers should contact the merchant first for issues such as items not received or wrong amounts. If the merchant does not respond within **15 days**, the customer can open a dispute.

## Unrecognised transactions (possible fraud)

1. **Freeze the card immediately** in the app (Cards → Freeze). Freezing is instant and reversible.
2. Open Help → **Report fraud**. Support will cancel the card and ship a replacement, which arrives in **5–7 business days**.
3. The customer receives a **provisional credit** within **2 business days** while the investigation runs.

## Timelines

- Disputes must be opened within **120 days** of the transaction date.
- Investigations take up to **45 days**. Complex cross-border cases can take up to **90 days**.
- If the investigation finds the transaction was authorised by the customer, the provisional credit is reversed with **10 days' notice**.

## What Support needs

Transaction ID, a short description, and any merchant correspondence. Support will **never** ask for the full card number, CVV or app PIN.
